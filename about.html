About PTSGI

Asia's leading language service provider, delivering world-class translation solutions for over half a century.

Who We Are

PTSGI is a leading global language service provider with over 50 years of experience in professional translation and localization. Ranked among the Top 5 worldwide by CSA Research, we serve thousands of enterprise clients across industries including technology, automotive, medical, legal, and finance.

Our network of 13,600+ carefully vetted translators covers 145+ languages, ensuring native-level fluency and deep domain expertise for every project. We operate 24/7 across multiple time zones to deliver fast, reliable results.

Quality is at our core — PTSGI holds ISO 9001, ISO 17100, and ISO 27001 certifications, guaranteeing the highest standards of quality management, translation services, and information security for every engagement.

Company Structure

PTSGI's organizational structure is designed to deliver seamless, end-to-end language solutions. Our specialized departments work in close coordination to ensure every project meets the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

Our Translation Department comprises expert linguists organized by industry vertical — including technology, medical, legal, automotive, and financial teams. The Interpretation Division handles simultaneous and consecutive interpretation for conferences, business meetings, and legal proceedings.

Project Management coordinates workflow, timelines, and client communication. Our Quality Assurance team enforces ISO-compliant review processes at every stage. The Technology Division develops and maintains proprietary tools including the GTAIS Intelligent Case Management System and AI-powered translation aids.
