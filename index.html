FAQ

Q.How does PTSGI select its translators?

PTSGI selects only native speakers with expertise in their respected fields.

Q.How is the translation cost calculated?

We usually charge based on the number of words in the source file. Prices vary depending on the difficulty and turnaround time.
Additional costs may be charged for recreating materials such as charts, tables, or images that require extra time, technical know-how, or labor.

Q.I want to check the quality level of your translation. Can I request a trial translation?

We offer free translations of about 300 words.

Q.Can I choose a translator to meet my quality requirements?

We offer trial translations from multiple translators, so that you can choose the translator that suits you best.
